Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. Applies to the website bloppit.com and the Bloppit app for iPhone and iPad.

Bloppit is made for children and families, so we collect as little as possible.

The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your photos and builds stay on your device.
  • The exceptions are rooms (Build together and live Blitz) and Blitz challenges. When you start one, the puzzle is shared through our server so your friends can play it. Rooms are deleted after 3 days without activity, and challenges 30 days after the last player.
  • The Daily Bloppit sends your time for the day and the name you play as (or “A player”, if you turn your name off), for the day’s scoreboard. It’s all deleted after a week.

What stays on your device

Photos you add, the brick models and booklets made from them, player profiles (a first name or nickname, and an age, which sets how hard the Brain Trail math puzzles are), scores, sound settings and build progress are all kept in your browser’s or the app’s own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by deleting a player in Bloppit, clearing your browser’s site data, or deleting the app.

The age is only used to choose how hard the math puzzles are. It never leaves your device.

Rooms (Build together and live Blitz)

Rooms let people on different devices build or race the same puzzle at the same time. To make that work, our server keeps a room with:

  • the room code;
  • the name each player types (we ask for a first name or nickname, and rude words are turned into “Player”);
  • the puzzle being built, including the photos it was made from;
  • the bricks each player places, their points or times, and room chat messages.

Only people who have the room code can ask to join, and the player who made the room decides who comes in. Chat is only shown to players in the room. On the website, chat messages are checked automatically: links, email addresses, phone numbers and social media names are refused, and rude words are hidden. The Bloppit app works the same way.

Voice chat (website only). The player who made a room can turn on voice chat, and each player chooses whether to join it. Audio goes directly between the players’ devices. Our server only passes along the short technical messages that let the devices connect, and audio is never recorded or stored. Voice chat is not available in the app, or in a room where someone is playing on the app.

A room is deleted automatically 3 days after its last activity. Our server does not use rooms for anything else.

Blitz challenges

A challenge lets a friend race your time later. When you make one, our server keeps the challenge code, the puzzle (including the photos it was made from), and the name and time of each player who races it (up to 12). Anyone with the code can see the names and times on that challenge. A challenge is deleted automatically 30 days after the last person played it.

Daily Bloppit

When you finish the Daily Bloppit for the first time each day, the app sends your time, the date and the player’s name, so it can tell you how you did compared with everyone else and show the day’s scoreboard. The scoreboard shows the fastest 50 names and times, and everyone who plays can see it. Names on the scoreboard must be a first name or nickname in plain letters: numbers, links and @handles aren’t allowed, and rude words become “Player”. You can turn “Show my name on the scoreboard” off on the Daily page, and you’ll show as “A player” instead. Nothing else is sent. Each day’s times and names are deleted after a week. Your streak and best times are kept on your device.

Information our hosting provider sees

Bloppit is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify’s servers briefly handle technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We don’t use this to identify or profile anyone. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its 3D library from public code libraries (cdnjs and jsDelivr), which also see the IP address of the device making the request. The app has these files built in, so it doesn’t contact these services.

Children

Bloppit is designed to be used by children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the player name typed into a room or a Blitz challenge, which is kept only for the life of that room or challenge, and the name shown on the Daily Bloppit scoreboard, which is deleted after a week (and can be turned off). In the app, anything that leads outside Bloppit, such as web links or sharing an invite, sits behind a grown-ups check.

Parents and guardians can ask us to delete a room or challenge at any time. Email us with its code and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change how Bloppit handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
