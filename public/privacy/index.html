Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026. Applies to the website bloppit.com and the Bloppit app for iPhone and iPad.

Bloppit is made for children and families, so we collect as little as possible.

The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your photos and builds stay on your device.
  • The one exception is Play together. When you start a room, the model is shared through our server so the other players can build it. Rooms are deleted after 3 days without activity.

What stays on your device

Photos you add, the brick models and booklets made from them, player profiles (a first name or nickname, and an age if you turn on the Math challenge), scores, sound settings and build progress are all kept in your browser’s or the app’s own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by deleting a player in Bloppit, clearing your browser’s site data, or deleting the app.

The age is only used to choose how hard the math puzzles are. It never leaves your device.

Play together

Play together lets people on different devices build the same model. To make that work, our server keeps a room with:

  • the room code;
  • the name each player types (we ask for a first name or nickname, and rude words are turned into “Player”);
  • the model being built, including the photos it was made from;
  • the bricks each player places, their points, and room chat messages.

Only people who have the room code can join, and chat is only shown to players in the room. On the website, chat messages are checked automatically: links, email addresses, phone numbers and social media names are refused, and rude words are hidden. In the Bloppit app, chat is limited to a fixed set of quick messages such as “Nice!” and “Your turn!”. Nobody can type their own messages in a room that has an app player in it.

Voice chat (website only). The player who made a room can turn on voice chat, and each player chooses whether to join it. Audio goes directly between the players’ devices. Our server only passes along the short technical messages that let the devices connect, and audio is never recorded or stored. Voice chat isn’t available in the Bloppit app, or in any room with an app player in it.

A room is deleted automatically 3 days after its last activity. Our server does not use rooms for anything else.

Information our hosting provider sees

Bloppit is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify’s servers briefly handle technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We don’t use this to identify or profile anyone. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its 3D library from public code libraries (cdnjs and jsDelivr), which also see the IP address of the device making the request. The app has these files built in, so it doesn’t contact these services.

Children

Bloppit is designed to be used by children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the player name typed into a Play together room, which is kept only for the life of that room. In the app, anything that leads outside Bloppit, such as web links or sharing an invite, sits behind a grown-ups check.

Parents and guardians can ask us to delete a Play together room at any time. Email us with the room code and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change how Bloppit handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
