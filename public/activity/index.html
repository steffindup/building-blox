Bloppit Activity

A ready-made 10–15 minute brick-building session for a class, a club, a library group or scouts. Works on school Chromebooks, shared iPads and restricted networks. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, nothing collected.

1

Pick a starter build

Three difficulties. Each link opens that exact build with one button, Build this one. Put the link on the board, or show the QR code on a screen.

Any model in the library works the same way: open it in Bloppit, and its link is bloppit.com/p/m- followed by its name in lower case with dashes. Bonus for fast finishers: Hide the booklet and build it again from memory, or race Scraps the Longtail in Blitz.

2

Run the session (10–15 minutes)

  1. 1 min · Open the link. Each child (or pair) opens the link on their device and taps Build this one. No name, no sign-in: if a menu appears instead, tap the yellow Start here card or the Quick Build tile.
  2. 1 min · The first brick. Tap the faded brick to snap the first one in. The booklet on the left shows each page; the parts box holds the bricks.
  3. 6–10 min · Build. A hint costs a star; a brick in the wrong spot bonks and bounces back. Three stars for a clean build. Nobody is timed.
  4. 2 min · Finish. The real picture appears behind the model. Compare stars, not speed. Fast finishers take the bonus challenge.
  5. 1 min · Reset. Reload the page, or tap Start over under the stage, and the device is ready for the next child.
3

Leader guide

  • Devices: any browser on a Chromebook, iPad, laptop or phone. The build itself works offline once the page has loaded; only the link needs the internet.
  • Pairs work well: one holds the booklet open, the other places the bricks, then swap. For a two-person build on two devices, use Relay with a friend on the Blitz page: one builds the first half, the other finishes it from a code.
  • Group goal: for a club that meets again, make a Crew on the Together page. Every brick anyone finishes adds to the crew’s lighthouse. The leader’s device makes the crew and lets each member in; nothing typed passes between members.
  • Stop and reset: reloading the page always returns a device to the start. Nothing is saved on the device unless a child finishes a build on it, and that can be cleared from the player menu (Who’s playing).
  • No upsell, no ads: there is nothing to buy anywhere in Bloppit.
4

Privacy, in short

Bloppit Activity collects nothing: no accounts, names, emails, class names, photos or rosters. Children open a link and build. If a child finishes a build, the device keeps an anonymous progress record; it is not linked to a person and a grown-up can delete it. Bloppit shows no ads, uses no trackers and sells nothing. The full privacy policy covers the optional features (crews, relays, the Weekly Bloppit counts) in detail. Bloppit is made by one person in Bermuda and is not affiliated with any toy brick brand.

For families afterwards, the take-home line is simply bloppit.com. A group that wants its own link to share with parents can use bloppit.com/go/act-YOURGROUP (any short word): Bloppit counts how many households arrived that way, and nothing else.
