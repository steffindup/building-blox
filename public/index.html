How to play

Pick a puzzle, grab the brick the booklet shows, and drag it into place. Build it clean for three stars, and every build you finish grows your island.

Meet Bloppit, your building buddy

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it.

Watch out for the Bermuda Longtails, the brick thieves you race in Blitz: Scraps is still learning, Swoop is quick but careless, and Squint hardly ever puts a brick in the wrong spot. Can you beat all three?

How it works

Your island

🏝️ Bloppitopia

A little otter arrives by boat, and your first build finishes its home. Every build after that grows your world: Home Island becomes a village, and five islands rise from the mist, Otterwood, Brainpeak, Sunrise Shores, Blitz Bay and Brickworks Island, each growing with its own game. Your otter levels up and earns outfits. Nothing is ever for sale.

⚡ Bloppit Blitz

A head-to-head brick race against the Longtails: Scraps is still learning, Swoop is quick but careless, Squint hardly ever misses. Take turns placing 1 to 3 bricks. Shortest clean time wins.

Start a race →

With friends: race a friend live or send a challenge code, or build one model together in a room. Friends only join with a code, and the host lets each one in. No chat, no voice: just bricks.
