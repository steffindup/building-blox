Meet Bloppit, your building buddy

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it.

Watch out for the Gulls, the brick thieves you race in Blitz: Scraps is still learning, Swoop is quick but careless, and Squint hardly ever puts a brick in the wrong spot. Can you beat all three?

How it works

Your island

🏝️ Otter Island

Every finished model lands on your own island. A hut rises after the first build, a workshop after three, a village after ten. Your pup grows with you, and Build Stars buy the decorations. Nothing is ever for sale.

Against the clock

⚡ Bloppit Blitz

A head-to-head brick race against the Gulls: Scraps is still learning, Swoop is quick but careless, Squint hardly ever misses. Take turns placing 1 to 3 bricks. Shortest clean time wins.

Start a race →

With friends: race a friend live or send a challenge code, or build one model together in a room. Friends only join with a code, and the host lets each one in. No chat, no voice: just bricks.
