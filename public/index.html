How it works

1

Snap it

Add any photo: your pet, your house, a holiday snap, a child’s drawing. A plain background works best.

2

Bloppit

Bloppit turns it into a brick model with a parts list and a step-by-step instruction booklet. You choose the size and how much detail.

3

Build it

Open the parts box, pick up the bricks you need and follow the booklet one step at a time, right on screen.

Three ways to bloppit

Your photos

Build your own

Turn the photos that matter to you into models: a birthday surprise, a holiday memory, the family dog.

Levels and points

Play the Game

Build each picture to finish the level. Levels start tiny and get bigger, and bigger builds are worth more points.

Meet Bloppit, your building buddy

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it.

Bloppit points at the next step in your booklet, holds up the bricks you need, and cheers every time a section clicks into place.
