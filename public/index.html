Your building buddy

Meet Bloppit

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it.

He builds beside you, cheers every brick, and looks after your own otter pup on Bloppitopia. Your pup grows as you play, earns outfits, and is always there to greet you when you come back.

How it works

Three steps, endless builds

  1. Pick a build

    A puzzle from one of the islands, any of the 66 models in the library, or your own photo or drawing turned into bricks.

  2. Build it brick by brick

    Follow the booklet, grab the brick it shows and tap it into place. Build it clean for three stars.

  3. Grow your world

    Every build you finish grows Bloppitopia: a home, a garden, a village, then five islands rising from the mist.

Bloppitopia

Five islands, five friends

Each island has its own way to play and a friend who lives there. Every build you finish there helps it grow.

Tiny the tree frog

Frog Forest

Hop up Tiny’s Frog Trail: 15 levels from tiny to tricky, with a boss at the top.

Nora the night heron

Brainpeak

The Nora’s Brain Trail: builds with a little maths in them, matched to the player’s age.

Pippa the penguin

Flipper Beach

Pippa’s Mirror Puzzles: she builds one half, and you build the reflection.

Swoop the Longtail

Blitz Bay

Race the speediest Bermuda Longtail, brick for brick. Win 19 races and the 20th is the Grand Prix.

Bibi the lion cub

Brickyard Cove

Turn your own photos and drawings into builds, and try Bibi’s Weekly Bloppit: one puzzle a week, the same for everyone.

Chapter 1 finale

Moonstone Isle

Finish all five islands and Moonstone Isle opens: ten chapters, one mystery, and someone small waiting to be found.

More ways to play

On your own or with friends

Bibi’s Weekly Bloppit

A new puzzle every Monday, the same for everybody. Build it clean, see how many builders did too, and keep your streak going.

The library

66 models in 11 themes, with a new one every week. Pick one and build at your own pace.

With friends

Play with Friends: a Team Build or Race a Friend, with a code to share. Or start a Crew: a Crew Quick Game live, Pass the Puzzle round the crew, and a Crew Mega Build over weeks.

For parents

Made for families

Bloppit is made in Bermuda by a dad of two boys, for kids and grown-ups who love bricks.

Free, for real

No ads, nothing to buy and no pay-to-win. Everything in the game is earned by building.

No chat, no strangers

Friends join only with a code, and the host lets each one in. There’s no chat and no voice: just bricks.

Private by default

No accounts and no tracking. Photos you turn into builds stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Plays anywhere

In any web browser, or as the app for iPhone and iPad, which also works offline.
