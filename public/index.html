Meet Bloppit, your building buddy

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it.

He’s also the one to beat. Pup Bloppit is still learning, Splash Bloppit is quick, and Legend Bloppit hardly ever puts a brick in the wrong spot. Can you beat all three?
