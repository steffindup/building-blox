About the game

Bloppit: World of Bricks is a brick-building game where every model you finish grows your own world, Bloppitopia.

Build with Bloppit the otter: snap bricks together page by page and finish a model in minutes. Every build grows your world. Your pup arrives by boat, your first build becomes its home, and five islands wait in the mist, each with a friend to meet. Plus a new puzzle every week.

Press contact: hello@bloppit.com

The story

Bloppit tells a story with a beginning and an ending. Chapter 1 is Bloppitopia. Your pup sails in, Bloppit is waiting on the jetty, and your first build becomes the pup’s home. Every build after that grows the world: a garden, a village, then five islands, each with a friend who needs your help.

There is a mystery too. A sixth island waits in the mist, and it opens only when the other five are complete. Its story unfolds over ten chapters and brings Chapter 1 to a real ending. Short films along the way tell the story, and you can watch them again whenever you like.

Chapter 2 is in the making. It picks up where the ending leaves off.
