Questions & answers

Everything grown-ups (and builders) usually ask.

Playing

Who is Bloppit for?

Bloppit is made for kids aged about 8 to 13, but grown-ups are very welcome, especially in Blitz. Younger builders can start on the first levels of Otter Trail or pick a small puzzle in Quick Build.

Does it cost anything?

No. Bloppit is free to play, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up and no email. Each player just picks a first name or nickname on the device, and their scores and progress are kept there.

What can I play it on?

Any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser: just go to bloppit.com/build. Bloppit for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Does it work offline?

Building, the Trails and racing Bloppit work without the internet once the page has loaded (and always in the app). Playing with friends, challenges and the Daily Bloppit need a connection.

What’s the difference between Otter Trail and Brain Trail?

They’re the same 14 levels. On Brain Trail, some booklet pages are locked behind a math puzzle, and the puzzles get harder as the levels go up. How hard they start depends on the age you enter for the player.

How does Bloppit Blitz work?

Players take turns placing 1, 2 or 3 bricks, then tap Done. Your clock only runs on your turn, everyone places the same number of bricks, and every brick in the wrong spot adds 3 seconds. Shortest total time wins. You can race Pup, Splash or Legend Bloppit, or a friend.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Add a photo in Quick Build and Bloppit turns it into a brick puzzle with its own booklet. A clear picture of one thing on a plain background works best. Try the flat Picture style if you want it to look more like the photo.

Can I build the models with real bricks?

Yes. Every finished puzzle has a Build it for real button that opens a printable booklet and parts list.

Friends and safety

How do kids play with friends?

One player makes a room and gets a short code. Friends type the code in (under Join a game) or open the share link. A room holds up to 4 players. Challenges work the same way, except friends can race your time whenever they like.

Is it safe for kids?
  • There’s no way to search for or message strangers: you can only join a game with its code.
  • Room chat refuses links, emails, phone numbers and social media names, blanks out rude words, and limits how fast messages can be sent. It’s a basic filter, not full moderation.
  • In the iPhone and iPad app, any link out of the app asks a grown-up question first.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
What happens to my photos?

Photos and builds stay on your device. The only time a photo leaves it is when you share that puzzle in a room or challenge, so your friends can play it. Rooms are deleted 3 days after they’re last used, and challenges 30 days after the last player. Full details are in the privacy policy.

What does the Daily Bloppit send?

Only your time for the day, with no name, so you can see where you rank. Times are deleted after a week.
