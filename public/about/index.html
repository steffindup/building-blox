About Bloppit

A small, independent brick puzzle game, made in Bermuda.

Made in Bermuda

Bloppit is a small, independent game made in Bermuda by a loving dad of two beautiful boys. It started with a simple idea: turn any photo into a brick model with a proper instruction booklet, the kind that comes in a real set, and build it yourself.

Then it grew into a game, with the Trails, Blitz, the Weekly Bloppit and Build together, so there’s always another puzzle to build, on your own or with friends.

What we care about

  • Building, not scrolling. Every puzzle is something you make, step by step, and can build for real afterwards.
  • Free and fair. No ads, nothing to buy, no pay-to-win.
  • Private by default. No accounts, no tracking, and your photos stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
  • Safe with friends. You only play with people who have your code.

Meet Bloppit

Real otters keep a favourite rock tucked under one arm. Bloppit keeps a favourite brick, and never builds without it. He’s your building buddy on the Trails, and the one to beat in Blitz.
