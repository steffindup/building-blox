Tap a brick to pick it up.

Drag bricks into segments to sort them. Drag a segment by its name bar to move it with its bricks, and by the corner to resize. To pick several: turn on Pick several (or press S), Shift-click, drag a box around them with the mouse, or use All on a segment. Press Enter to take them to the build.

    Saved progress

    Bloppit saves scores and dates under an anonymous progress code. No name, age, photo or device details are ever stored. Privacy policy

    Move this player to another device

    Make a transfer code here, then on the other device open Who’s playing → Grown-ups → Bring a player here and type it in. A code works once and lasts 72 hours.

    Family key

    A family key is your own 12-character key, shown once: keep it in your password manager. Players attached to it can be seen, brought to any device or deleted from the server with the key alone, with no transfer code. Nothing about you is stored, only which players belong together.

    Family Link: send Bloppit to another device

    Scan this with the child’s iPad, phone or Chromebook, or send the link. It opens Bloppit’s first build. Nothing personal crosses. To bring this player’s progress along too, make a transfer code above first; it is added to the link while it lasts.
