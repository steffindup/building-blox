How it works

Pick a puzzle, build it from the booklet, and play it your way.

1

Pick a puzzle

Choose one of about 60 ready-made puzzles in 10 themes, with a new one every week, from dragons and race cars to a Bermuda moongate, or this week’s Weekly Bloppit. Or snap a photo of your own (your pet, your house, a drawing) and Bloppit turns it into a brick picture made of tiles, right on your device.

Photo tips: one main thing in the picture and a plain background work best. You choose how many tiles; more tiles look more like the photo. Photos never leave your device.

2

Build it from the booklet

Every puzzle comes with a step-by-step instruction booklet and a parts box, just like a real brick set. Each step shows the bricks it needs. Pick a brick from the box and click it into its spot on the model. Bloppit points at the next step and cheers when one is finished.

Stuck? Ask for a hint, or tap “place one for me”. On the Trails each brick you place scores 10 points, and hints cost a few. A brick in the wrong spot just gives a friendly bonk, so try again.

3

Play your way

The same building, lots of ways to play:

🦦 On your own

Quick Build: any puzzle, at your own pace. Otter Trail: 14 levels, from tiny to tricky. Brain Trail: 14 different levels, with math puzzles that unlock booklet pages, set to the player’s age.

⚡ Against the clock

Bloppit Blitz: take turns placing 1 to 3 bricks against the Longtails (Scraps, Swoop or Squint), or a friend. A hint adds 5 seconds, and a brick in the wrong spot adds 1 second. Weekly Bloppit: one puzzle a week for everyone, scored on how clean you build.

🧩 Together

Build together: make a room, share the code, and up to 4 players take turns building one model. Or send a challenge code and see who builds it fastest.

4

Grow your world

Every build you finish grows Bloppitopia. A little otter arrives by boat, and your first build finishes its home. Home Island grows into a village, and five islands rise from the mist as you play: Otterwood (Otter Trail), Brainpeak (Brain Trail), Sunrise Shores (Weekly Bloppit), Blitz Bay (Blitz) and Brickworks Island (your own creations). Your otter levels up and earns outfits, and Build Stars buy decorations. Nothing is ever for sale.

5

Build it for real

Loved a puzzle? Every finish has a Build it for real button. Print the booklet and parts list, then build it on the table with your own bricks.
