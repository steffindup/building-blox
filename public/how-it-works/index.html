How it works

Pick a puzzle, build it from the booklet, and play it your way.

1

Pick a puzzle

Choose from 60+ ready-made puzzles in 11 themes, with a new one every week, from dragons and race cars to a Bermuda moongate, or Bibi’s Weekly Bloppit. Or snap a photo of your own (your pet, your house, a drawing) and Bloppit turns it into a brick picture made of tiles, right on your device.

Photo tips: one main thing in the picture and a plain background work best. You choose how many tiles; more tiles look more like the photo. Photos never leave your device.

2

Build it from the booklet

Every puzzle comes with a step-by-step instruction booklet and a parts box, just like a real brick set. Each step shows the bricks it needs. Pick a brick from the box and tap it into its spot on the model. Bloppit points at the next step and cheers when one is finished.

Stuck? Ask for a hint, or tap “place one for me”. Every build starts with stars: five for an easy one, eight for a medium one and ten for a hard one. A brick that needs a hint, a “place one for me” or another try costs one star, and only one, however tricky it is, and a finished build always keeps at least two. A brick in the wrong spot just gives a friendly bonk, so try again.

3

Play your way

The same building, lots of ways to play:

On your own

Quick Play: a random puzzle, right now, just for fun (nothing is added to Bloppitopia). Build Anything with Bibi: any puzzle, at your own pace. Tiny’s Frog Trail: 15 levels, from tiny to tricky, the last one a boss. Nora’s Brain Trail: 15 different levels, with maths puzzles that unlock booklet pages, set to the player’s age. Pippa’s Mirror Puzzles: 15 puzzles where Pippa builds one half and you build its mirror image. Pippa also sends a message in a bottle once a day, with a fun fact and a bonus star. From Memory: hide the booklet and finish without hints for two bonus stars.

Against the clock

Blitz with Swoop: take turns placing 1 to 3 bricks against Swoop the longtail (Warm-up, Steady or Full throttle; 20 races, the last one the Grand Prix). A hint adds 2 seconds, and a brick in the wrong spot adds 1 second.

Every week

Bibi’s Weekly Bloppit: one new puzzle a week, the same for everyone, scored on how clean you build, never on time.

Together

Play with Friends: a Team Build (make a room, share the code, and up to 4 players take turns building one model) or Race a Friend, live or with a challenge code. Your Crews: a group of friends you keep, with a Crew Quick Game to play live, Pass the Puzzle (one model passed round, each building their pages in turn) and the Crew Mega Build.

4

Grow your world

Every build you finish grows Bloppitopia. A little otter arrives by boat, and your first build finishes its home. Home Island grows into a village, and five islands rise from the mist as you play: Frog Forest (Tiny’s Frog Trail), Brainpeak (Nora’s Brain Trail), Flipper Beach (Pippa’s Mirror Puzzles, which wash ashore as your world grows), Blitz Bay (Blitz) and Brickyard Cove (your own creations and Bibi’s Weekly Bloppit). Finish all five and Moonstone Isle opens: a ten-chapter mystery that ends Chapter 1, and then a voyage to a new world, where Chapter 2 is being built. Stars are the one thing to collect: they level up your otter, which grows and earns outfits, and every star is also a Galaxy Block to spend on decorations in Bibi’s shop. Nothing is ever for sale.
