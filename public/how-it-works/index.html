How it works

Pick a puzzle, build it from the booklet, and play it your way.

1

Pick a puzzle

Choose one of 64 ready-made puzzles in 11 themes, from dragons and race cars to a Bermuda moongate. Or snap a photo of your own (your pet, your house, a drawing) and Bloppit turns it into bricks.

Photo tips: one main thing in the picture and a plain background work best. You choose how big the model is and whether it’s a 3D model or a flat Picture made of tiles, which looks more like the photo.

2

Build it from the booklet

Every puzzle comes with a step-by-step instruction booklet and a parts box, just like a real brick set. Each step shows the bricks it needs. Pick a brick from the box and click it into its spot on the model. Bloppit points at the next step and cheers when one is finished.

Stuck? Ask for a hint, or tap “place one for me”. On the Trails each brick you place scores 10 points, and hints cost a few. A brick in the wrong spot just gives a friendly bonk, so try again.

3

Play your way

The same building, lots of ways to play:

🦦 On your own

Quick Build: any puzzle, at your own pace. Otter Trail: 14 levels, from tiny to tricky. Brain Trail: the same levels, with math puzzles that unlock booklet pages, set to the player’s age.

⚡ Against the clock

Bloppit Blitz: take turns placing 1 to 3 bricks against Pup, Splash or Legend Bloppit, or a friend. A hint adds 5 seconds, and a brick in the wrong spot adds 1 second. Daily Bloppit: one puzzle a day for everyone.

🧩 Together

Build together: make a room, share the code, and up to 4 players take turns building one model. Or send a challenge code and see who builds it fastest.

4

Build it for real

Loved a puzzle? Every finish has a Build it for real button. Print the booklet and parts list, then build it on the table with your own bricks.

Ready to build? Let’s bloppit!
