# Weekly clips (draft, for Steff's approval): Moon rocket (30 Nov) and Ringed planet (7 Dec)

Made 18 Nov 2026 like the turkey clip (`../turkey-clip-2026-11-23/`): the Weekly built brick by brick, the special
piece last (the flame rises from below; the ring sweeps across), then an end card with that week's Bloppit picture
(`../weekly-art/`). About 10 seconds, the launch video's music, upright and wide.

`how-it-was-made/compose2.py` is the turkey composer made general (`CFG=rocket.json python3 compose2.py upright out.mp4`).
Captures: build in preview with "fit the whole build" on and the result card hidden; place bricks until all but one
are in (`bloppitPlacedBoxes`, not a fixed count: fast clicks drop bricks); the camera zooms on the special piece's spot
for the last step, so the before picture is taken with the whole-build view switched on again.
