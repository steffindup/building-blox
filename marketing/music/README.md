# Music

- `bloppit-spotlight-cue-14.8s.wav`: the soundtrack of the Friday spotlights (Meet Tiny, Meet Swoop), approved by Steff
  on 18 Nov 2026. Written in code (`spotlight-cue.py`, then `ffmpeg -af loudnorm=I=-15:TP=-1.5:LRA=9`) to the video's
  three parts: marimba and pad under the film (0-4.6 s), bass and a light beat under the gameplay (4.6-11.1 s), a run-up,
  chime and one final chord on the end card (11.1-14.8 s). C major, 104 BPM. Change the scene times at the top of the
  script for another video. Replaced the launch track for these because its own sections (one starts about 12 s in)
  sounded "jumpy" against the cuts.
- The launch, Weekly and Play with Friends videos use the launch video's track (`../launch-video/`).
