# lufu on Moonstone Isle

Load lufu.glb with Three.js GLTFLoader and create a LufuController with the resulting gltf object. Add gltf.scene to the island and call controller.update(deltaSeconds) each frame. Use controller.play('offer') when the pup holds out its brick in the finale. Place the characters and camera together in the actual scene to align the exchange. The files deliver the character performance. Chapter unlocks, the spoken finale line, the boat journey, and Home Island placement belong to the game.

Idle loops. Curiosity, awe, reverence, sorrow, cheer, offer and blink return to idle through the controller. The two-stud galaxy stone follows the offering paw. The wisp is a separate floating element. The original gold is the default. The controller coordinates the eyes, pendant and wisp with the expression colours in the character study. The standalone GLB carries the gold material and all eight motion clips.

The studio reference comes from the original lufu identity frame locked on November 3. Meshy supplied one private textured base. Local work retained the face, ears, scarf, pendant and feet, removed the generated tail, rebuilt the flanks and short arms, fitted closing lids, and added the separate wisp and two-stud moonstone.

The interactive review places the original identity frame beside the animated model. The QC folder includes checks on the exact GLB, skinned motion at 60 Hz, prop contact and ear clearance at 15 Hz, closed and reopened eyes, transitions in the Three.js mixer, and multi-angle motion samples. Eyelid surfaces and translucent light cards are intentionally open. The editable Blender file contains the authored rig. The clean export starts each clip at zero seconds.

The user approved and locked v2 with the galaxy stone. Rendering in the live island, paired placement with the pup and target-device performance are pending. The warm Blender review renders show the default gold material. The expression palette is driven by the Three.js controller.
